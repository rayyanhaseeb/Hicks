## 2. Usage Rules

*   Use **Woody White** for light surfaces, general page backgrounds, and light-themed cards.
*   Use **Charcoal Black** for dark surfaces, page loaders, footers, and dark-themed wrappers (such as the booking form card).
*   Maintain high-contrast legibility: white text/borders on **Charcoal Black** surfaces, and black text/borders on **Woody White** surfaces.
